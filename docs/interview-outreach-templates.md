# Outreach Templates — Recruiting Interviewees

Target: 5–10 interviews across developer friends, small AI teams, AI startups, teams shipping LLM features (`PRD.md` §31). Aim for at least a couple from each of the three candidate beachhead segments (general AI dev teams, legal-tech, fintech) if you can reach any — even 1–2 legal/fintech contacts will help sanity-check the verification-domain decision.

Keep every message short. The ask is a 20-minute call, not a sales pitch — don't describe the product in detail here, save that for the interview itself (see `user-interview-guide.md` — don't pitch before asking questions).

---

## 1. Developer friends (casual, Slack/DM/text)

> Hey [name] — I'm building something in the LLM infra space (model routing + catching AI errors before they ship) and want to sanity-check it against how people actually work before I go further. Got 20 min this week or next for a quick call? Not pitching you anything, mostly want to hear how you currently deal with picking models / dealing with bad outputs.

## 2. Small AI teams / startups (LinkedIn or cold email, slightly more formal)

**Subject:** Quick question about your LLM setup (20 min?)

> Hi [name],
>
> I'm working on a tool for routing tasks across different LLMs and catching errors before they reach users, and I'm talking to teams who ship LLM-powered features to understand current pain points before building further.
>
> Would you have 20 minutes in the next week or two for a quick call? I'm not selling anything at this stage — just trying to understand how teams like yours currently decide which model to use and what happens when something goes wrong.
>
> Happy to work around your schedule.
>
> [your name]

## 3. Warm intro request (to send to someone who might refer you)

> Hey [name] — quick favor. I'm doing user research for an LLM infra tool (routing + error-catching) and looking for people who build production LLM features to talk to for 20 min. Know anyone on your team, or elsewhere, who'd be a good fit? Happy to send them a short note directly if you can make the intro.

## 4. Follow-up if no response after ~5 days

> Hey [name] — following up in case this got buried. Still hoping to grab 20 min if you have it; totally understand if timing's not right, no worries either way.

## 5. Thank-you / close-out (send within a day of the call)

> Thanks again for the time today, [name] — really helpful. I'll keep you posted on where this goes. [If they agreed to be a design partner: "I'll reach out once there's something worth trying — appreciate you offering."]
